Live Demo & Pitch Script 2-Minute Walkthrough

Step-by-step speaker script for presenting to evaluators, judges, or civic leaders

1

The Problem: Unverified Complaints & Persistent Black Spots

"Bengaluru generates over 5,500 tonnes of municipal waste daily. Traditional complaint portals suffer from spam, single-person false reports, and zero consensus. BBMP waste trucks are sent on wild goose chases while genuine chronic black spots linger for weeks."

2

The Innovation: 50m Community Consensus Engine

"Namma Clean Bengaluru introduces a crowd-consensus threshold: any new dump starts as a Yellow Pin (Pending 1/5). Only when 5 nearby residents within a 50m radius independently confirm the spot does our algorithm mark it as TRUE and escalate."

3

Live Transition Demo (Pending ➔ Verified Red Pin)

"Look at Koramangala 4th Block (currently at 4/5). When one more neighbor clicks '+1 Verify'—or we trigger our bulk simulation—the engine instantly validates the spot, switches the marker to a pulsing Crimson Red Pin, generates official BBMP Ticket #BBMP-BLR-xxxx, and notifies the South Zone SWM Marshall."

4

The Municipal Side: BBMP Marshall Console

"BBMP Marshalls switch into their dedicated console. They only see verified hotspots sorted by urgency. Once cleared, they upload photographic proof, turning the red pin into an Emerald Green pin and archiving the result into our public 'Swachh Wins' Before/After gallery."

5

Gamification & Ward Accountability

"Citizens earn Namma Civic Points (+50 for report, +20 for verification, +100 for verified cleanup) unlocking perks like BMTC pass discounts. Meanwhile, the Ward Leaderboard holds local corporators and ward marshalls publicly accountable."
